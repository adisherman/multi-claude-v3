# 2. Objectives and Success Criteria
- Ship a polished, crash‑free MVP playable within 10 seconds of cold start.
- Deliver two modes: Solo vs CPU and Local Pass‑and‑Play.
- Maintain WCAG AA basics: contrast, touch targets, focus order (where applicable on mobile).
- Stability: ≥ 99.5% crash‑free sessions in internal testing.
